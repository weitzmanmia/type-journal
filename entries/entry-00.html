THIS IS WHERE I WOULD TALK ABOUT THE TYPE BLAH BLAH BLAH... Heavy weight up top for the dish names, a lighter, looser script underneath for prices, so the eye lands on food first, cost second. Tight line spacing keeps a long list from feeling empty on the board, and the slight upward slope of each line gives it energy instead of looking sloppy. It's a reminder that hierarchy doesn't need three typefaces — weight and scale alone can carry the whole system.
