The Journal

A semester-long hunt for good (and bad) type.

Every week I go looking for one typographic specimen in the wild — a sign, a menu, a label, a tattoo, a parking ticket — and write about what it's doing, why it works or doesn't, and what it says about the taste I'm building as a designer.

Each entry pairs an original photograph with 50–100 words of analysis: the typeface (or my best guess), its classification, what's happening with spacing and hierarchy, how it's paired with other type, the material it lives on, and the context around it.
