The Journal

A semester-long hunt for good (and bad) type.

My goal is to analyze a real-world piece of type once a week, whether it be from restaurant menus, product labels, tattoos, parking tickets or even a random street sign. The ultimate goal is to figure out why it works or fails, and track how my own eye as a designer is evolving.

Each entry will have a photograph of what I found along with an pairs an analysis about the typeface (or my best guess), its classification, what's happening with spacing and hierarchy, how it's paired with other type, the material it lives on, and the context around it.
